## Annotated usage

### `@luc/ui/app-shell`

- Use: Landmarks, sidebar and injected navigation.
- Avoid: Do not import a router into the library.
- Accessibility: Landmarks, skip link and current navigation are built in.

### `@luc/ui/charts`

- Use: Prepared series, generic chart composition and synchronization.
- Avoid: Do not calculate indicators or import engine internals.
- Accessibility: Provide a ChartDataTable alternate for canvas content.

### `@luc/ui/components/button`

- Use: Actions and native button behavior; use buttonVariants for links.
- Avoid: Do not use Button as a polymorphic link or assume asChild exists.
- Accessibility: Native button; loading reports aria-busy.

### `@luc/ui/components/input`

- Use: Text input with shared control chrome and Field integration.
- Avoid: Textarea and SearchInput are root exports, not this subpath.
- Accessibility: Pair with Field or provide an accessible name.

### `@luc/ui/components/select`

- Use: Choose one known form value.
- Avoid: Do not use for navigation or an action menu.
- Accessibility: Name the trigger via Field or aria-label.

### `@luc/ui/data-table`

- Use: Semantic tables with controlled sorting, filtering and pagination.
- Avoid: Not an editable grid or financial calculation engine.
- Accessibility: Native table, headers and labelled controls.

### `@luc/ui/experiences`

- Use: Page-level assemblies: profile hero, social links, career timeline, project showcase, publication list, reading shelf, now section.
- Avoid: Do not import a router, a data source or a chart engine; these are presentational and take their content as props.
- Accessibility: Landmarks, ordered lists, labelled links and progress meters are built in; pass localized strings for the empty and filter labels.

### `@luc/ui/financial-chart`

- Use: Prepared OHLC and financial time series.
- Avoid: Do not treat provider widgets as a source for prepared series.
- Accessibility: Use a semantic data alternate and preserve attribution.

### `@luc/ui/motion`

- Use: Opt-in interaction effects under the motion intensity contract.
- Avoid: Do not animate every resting surface.
- Accessibility: Respect reduced motion and preserve visible resting content.

### `@luc/ui/tradingview`

- Use: Typed TradingView widget wrappers.
- Avoid: Do not scrape or recolor provider internals.
- Accessibility: Provider iframe and shadow content are outside LUC's a11y boundary.

### `@luc/ui/workspace`

- Use: Resizable panes and inspector layouts.
- Avoid: Not arbitrary docking or drag-and-drop dashboard layout.
- Accessibility: Resize separators expose keyboard and ARIA behavior.
